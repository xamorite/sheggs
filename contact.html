Before you write in

A few
answers.

How far in advance should I book?

Weddings and large editorial jobs are usually booked 3–6 months out, especially for peak season dates. Portrait sessions can often be arranged within 1–2 weeks.

Do you travel outside Nigeria?

Yes — international and destination bookings are welcome. Travel, flights and accommodation are quoted separately once the date and location are confirmed.

How long until I get my photos?

A curated sneak-peek edit goes out within 72 hours. Full galleries are delivered within 3–4 weeks for weddings, and 1–2 weeks for portrait and editorial sessions.

Do you offer prints or albums?

Yes — fine-art prints and handmade albums are available as an add-on to any package. Details are shared with your final gallery delivery.
