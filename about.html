Cinematographer · Photographer · Equipment Rentals

I don’t just capture moments — I create visual experiences.

I’m a Cinematographer, Photographer, and Equipment Rental Professional driven by a passion for turning ideas into striking visual stories. From the energy of music videos and the precision of commercials to the raw emotions of events, I approach every project with a cinematic eye and a commitment to detail.

My work is built around storytelling, lighting, composition, movement, and atmosphere — creating visuals that don’t just look good, but make people feel something.

Alongside production, I provide professional camera, lighting, and production equipment for rent, giving creators and production teams access to reliable gear to bring their vision to life.

Your vision. My lens. One story at a time.

How it started

A timeline.

2017

First borrowed camera

Started shooting friends and campus events on a secondhand body, learning light by trial and error rather than tutorials.

2019

First paid wedding

Booked as a second shooter for a friend's wedding — the brief was simple, the lesson wasn't: people relax when you stop asking them to pose.

2021

Studio Noir campaign

First editorial commission for a fashion label, shot entirely on available light — the beginning of the studio's monochrome series.

2023

Sheqqs Lens founded

Turned freelance work into a studio with a real booking calendar, a second shooter on call, and a proper archive system.

2026

84 frames and counting

120+ weddings, a growing editorial client list, and work carried across 14 countries — still shot the same unhurried way.
